Read-only account

{{ how('credentials', 'read-account') }}

A second account per device, for reads only. The AI's device reads sign in with it only when Settings › AI's “AI device reads sign in as” is the read-only account (hardened); by default they sign in as Mercury's account, as Show commands does. With it they never escalate. It is looked for in the profiles' order (platform, kind, site, default): the first profile that carries one gives it. Under that choice, a device none covers is refused by name, “no read-only account for <device>”, and nothing is read from it.
