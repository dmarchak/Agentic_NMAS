Read-only account

{{ how('credentials', 'read-account') }}

A second account per device, for reads only. Every AI read signs in with it, never with the deploy account, and never escalates. It is looked for in the profiles' order (platform, kind, site, default): the first profile that carries one gives it. A device none covers is refused by name, “no read-only account for <device>”, and nothing is read from it.
