The card's own "The day begins at" field is gone: one zone decides every day boundary.

Until the day moves, the AI chip, the cap's refusal and every "resets" read the same 30-hour day from one function (the day boundary's one home, modules/ai/calls.py), so a screen and the counter cannot disagree. After 4 Nov 00:00 CST the chip on the "day moves" card disappears.

Decisions: each taken at its recommendation under the Phase 7 mode, one line in docs/STANDING_APPROVAL_LOG.md.

D1 · Where the setting lives

  1. Recommended: a General tab, first, on Settings › Installation. The zone is the installation's, like who may sign in.
  2. A field on the Default network's settings: a network would inherit it, and two networks could draw one event at two times.

D2 · The viewer's browser zone

  1. Recommended: no longer used. Every script that redraws a time in the browser's zone (the clock words, Logs' query, the panels) draws in the installation's zone, so one event has one time on every screen and in every person's report. A person's own zone comes later, with accounts.
  2. Keep the browser's zone where it is used today: two people read two times for one event, the defect C1740 records.

D3 · The AI day cap's own zone (ai_cap_day_boundary)

  1. Recommended: it follows the display zone, and its own field leaves the AI card for a line naming the display zone. Its stored value stays readable for one release (expand, then contract). A release where it was set to a zone and no display zone is set seeds the display zone from it, so the cap's day stays where it was put.
  2. Keep two zones: the chip's "today" and the cap's day could disagree with every other "today".

D4 · When a change moves the day

  1. Recommended: displayed times move at Save. The day boundary moves at the first midnight in the new zone after Save, so the day being counted is one longer or shorter day, its length said on the card and in the preview. No day is counted twice, and no spend falls outside a day.
  2. Move the boundary at Save: today's spend would be counted again or lost in a day that began hours ago.

D5 · How a time names its zone

  1. Recommended: the zone database's abbreviation after the time (CST, CDT), its offset where it has none (UTC+05:30), UTC on hover, and a table naming it once in its column head.
  2. The offset everywhere: always exact, and harder to read at a glance.

D6 · Who may change it, and its record

  1. Recommended: an admin as a verified person, as every installation setting is, recorded on History with both zones and when the day moves. No reason asked: the change is reversible and moves no stored data.
