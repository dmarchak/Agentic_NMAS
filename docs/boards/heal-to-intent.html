Heal to intent (board H): board for the operator's review

The operator's direction of 2026-11-03, for Lab 10: a self-healing application that heals a device back to its committed intent, deterministic and with no AI in the loop. A can't-fix notification carries an advisory AI analysis (the addition of the same day). The design, the tier table, the guards, the demo plan, the estimate and the schedule are in docs/HEAL_TO_INTENT.md. Seven artboards at 1440 wide, then the decisions. Yellow dashed notes are the board's annotations, not part of the screen. Every other link and control is one the built screen will have. Not self-signed: the operator reviews this before anything is built.

Decisions for the operator: only what industry practice does not decide. Each has its recommendation; the reasons are in docs/HEAL_TO_INTENT.md section 18.

N1 · The grace period

  1. Recommended: 900 s by default (section 7's approved "2 readings and 15 minutes"); 120 s on a network marked lab, so a demo heal lands before the downstream alerts.

N2 · A trigger beyond the three named

  1. Recommended: any firing alert on a managed device checks that device. M4's own CONFIG_I never reaches Mercury; without this, M4 heals only at the 4-hour schedule.

N3 · The topology-change window

  1. Recommended: 60 minutes after any Mercury change record on the device or an intent neighbour, or an observed link-set change.

N4 · Which ACLs count as security

  1. Recommended: all of them, definitions, entries and every binding, with no exception list in the first slice.

N5 · The approved state

  1. Recommended: the last golden that matched intent (Intent-Match: yes), not a baseline tag.

N6 · N per hour

  1. Recommended: section 7's approved rails: 1 per device and 6 per network an hour; the same fix twice in 24 h halts.

N7 · R3 on r6 Gi2, Mercury's path to r6

  1. Recommended: run R3's heal on the r3–r4 link's Gi4, and keep the management-interface refusal.

N8 · AUTO's expiry, and marking the network lab

  1. Recommended: an expiry is required, at most 7 days, then dry run. Marking Default lab and turning AUTO on there are the operator's.

N9 · A line in a secret position

  1. Recommended: removing a routing key is Tier 1, masked, with the policy's words as its reason; adding any secret back is Tier 2; accounts, AAA and communities are Tier 4.

N10 · The analysis's pack carries the masked intent-diff lines

  1. Recommended: send them, as Explain does (at most 20, the allowed class of 2026-10-29). The step log's own lines stay by kind.

N11 · Phase 8's end

  1. Recommended: it moves to about Dec 11, with 0.1 unchanged; the credit-bound measurement rounds stay before 11-29.

N12 · A change a named person made at the CLI

  1. Recommended: heal after the grace period, naming the person and notifying; the person-working guard holds a device under active work.
