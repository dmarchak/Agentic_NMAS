{# ── NetBox write safety ──────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
NetBox write safety
Writes are off by default. NMAS reads NetBox freely; creating, updating, or deleting objects requires this switch. Import shows a preview and can enable writes from its confirm dialog, so you do not have to set this first.
NMAS tags everything it creates nmas-managed and records its IDs. Removal only ever touches objects matching both.
