Credential profiles

{%- if v.state != 'unreadable' and op.state != 'form' %} Add a profile…{{ how('credentials', 'profiles', compact=True, op='a profile') }} {%- endif %}

A device a NetBox inventory brings in carries no login of its own. The tool gives it one, in this order: the device's own login (set by onboarding, adopt or a rotation), then its network's credential list, then a profile for its role, then for its site, then the default profile. A local inventory's devices carry their login in it and never read a profile.
