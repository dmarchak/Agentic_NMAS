**No read of the host was made for this script:** the agent's read-only look is owed the day of

interface, in your browser, except three lab steps (L1 to L3: starting, restarting and

**The throwaway, and why it is not a switch.** The repository holds no node called s5: the lab's
topology on `<lab-host>` has r1 to r5 and s1 to s4 (lab/REDEPLOY.md), r6 has a lab of its own, and the
only throwaway the repository can bring up is the one C8000v of
`docs/bootstrap-probe/nmas-throwaway.clab.yml` (its node `tw-ztp-a`, with its BGP peer `tw-frr`),
the lab docs/THROWAWAY_SESSION.md ran on 2026-10-05 and 06. It boots with no configuration and
takes its first one over ZTP, served by Mercury, so no secret ever passes through a file you
handle. A vIOS switch cannot do that here: its ZTP path was never measured in this lab, and a
static onboarding would mean copying a revealed credential into a startup file by hand. So the
throwaway is that C8000v, booted twice and named in Mercury once per boot:

- **s6** (boot 1) is created, abandoned before Verify, and then **adopted** with the login its
  first-boot config gave it: adopt's first real run (board G).
- **s5** (boot 2) is created and **onboarded** by Verify, then seeded, deployed to, broken,
  rolled back, retried and reverted: C148's acceptance and CUTOVER rows 52 and 58.

The names are Mercury's; the node is the same container both times, and its role is router.

**Revert by reload is not built** (docs/NSOT_REVERT_BY_RELOAD.md: "Nothing here is built"; the
three boards on the page "Phase 2: Revert by reload" are **later** rows in
docs/fidelity/device.md). CUTOVER row 52's revert is **Revert intent…**: one forward commit,
nothing sent, nothing restarted. No device in this sitting is reloaded or restarted by Mercury.
