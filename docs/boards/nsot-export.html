Committing a network's NSOT export: board for sign-off

Seven artboards (A to F at 1440, G at 390). The NSOT export is what NetBox says about a network at a moment, one deterministic document committed in the network's configuration repository; SDN › Apps and the controller app deploy read the controller hosts and the expected switches and links from the committed one, never NetBox live. This is its writer: a control on SDN › Apps that reads NetBox now, previews the export's hash and what changed against the committed one, and commits it when a verified person confirms by that hash. It changes no device and writes nothing to NetBox. Yellow dashed notes are annotations. Every other link and control is one the built card has.
