Heal to intent (board H): board for the operator's review

The operator's direction of 2026-11-03, for Lab 10: a self-healing application that heals a device back to its committed intent, deterministic and with no AI in the loop. A can't-fix notification carries an advisory AI analysis (the addition of the same day). Not self-signed: the operator reviews this before anything is built.
